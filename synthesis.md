# Synthesis

Based on 3 interviews (`discovery/interview-1.md`, `interview-2.md`, `interview-3.md`). Each claim lists who said it.

## What people said and did

1. **People cook at home often.** About 1 to 3 times a day. (Interviews 1, 2, 3)
2. **Cooking gets repetitive.** One person doesn't enjoy it because of that. (Interview 1)
3. **People cook the same basic things.** One person mostly makes rice and ground beef. (Interview 2)
4. **People want more variety.** One person said they'd like to cook more different things. (Interview 2)
5. **Some ingredients get wasted.** One person had ingredients go to waste because they didn't know what to do with them. (Interview 3)

## Patterns

- Repetition and not knowing what to make came up in all three interviews, in different words.
- Only one person talked about wasted ingredients, so that's a lead, not a pattern yet.
