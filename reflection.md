# Reflection

This project was fun and engaging. Interviewing people gave me good feedback. I got some responses that I expected from college students, which was good. I can't wait to build this out. For example, one person said they eat ground beef and rice, and it made me wonder if TikTok has influenced them, because that's a heavily recommended meal for people on TikTok. Next time I would ask people where they get inspiration for the meals they cook.
