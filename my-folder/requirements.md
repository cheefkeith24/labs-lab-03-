# Requirements (first draft)

Based only on 3 interviews. See `interview-1.md`, `interview-2.md`, `interview-3.md`.

## User
People who cook at home regularly (once to several times a day) and run out of ideas for what to make.

## Problem
Home cooks repeat the same few meals and sometimes waste ingredients because they don't know what to do with them.

## Evidence

| What we heard | Source | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| Cooking gets repetitive | Interview 1 | said |
| Mostly cooks the same basic food (rice and ground beef) | Interview 2 | did |
| Would like to cook more different things | Interview 2 | said |
| Some ingredients went to waste because they didn't know what to do with them | Interview 3 | did |
| All three cook at home often (about 1 to 3 times a day) | Interviews 1, 2, 3 | did |

## Requirements
1. The user can enter or pick ingredients they already have. (Interview 3)
2. The app suggests meals that use those ingredients. (Interviews 2, 3)
3. Suggestions include meals other than the user's usual ones, so cooking feels less repetitive. (Interviews 1, 2)
4. The user can mark an ingredient as "need to use soon" and get ideas for it first. (Interview 3)

## What the interviews do not support yet
- Nobody was asked what they currently do to find ideas (recipe sites, videos, friends), so we don't know what this has to beat.
- Nobody was asked if they ever paid for or downloaded anything to solve this.
- Only one person mentioned wasted ingredients.
- Three interviews is a small sample, and we don't know how the interviewees were chosen.

## Questions for the next round
- Last time you didn't know what to cook, what did you actually do?
- Have you ever tried an app, site, or service for meal ideas? What happened?
- When did you last throw away food? What was it?
